# Privacy Policy **The Archive Clipping Studio** **Last updated:** October 20, 2026 The Archive Clipping Studio is a locally operated application developed by Princ3Studi0s to help content creators manage video clips and social media publishing workflows. ## Information We Process The application may process video recordings, extracted clips, publishing titles and captions, scheduled publishing information, and social media account authorization data. ## Connected Social Accounts When a user connects a supported social media account, the application requests authorization through the platform's official login system. Authorization tokens are stored locally in encrypted form for use by the application. ## Data Storage Video recordings, approved clips, publishing information, and application settings are primarily stored on the user's local computer. Data may be transmitted to social media platforms when the user authorizes an integration or initiates a supported platform operation. ## Data Sharing The application does not sell personal information. Information is shared with connected services only as needed for authorized functionality. ## Data Retention and Deletion Users may disconnect social accounts through the application's connection settings. Locally stored recordings and application data may require separate deletion. Disconnecting an account does not necessarily delete content already published on an external platform. ## Security The application uses local encryption for stored social authorization tokens. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. ## Contact For privacy questions, contact the developer through the official Princ3Studi0s channels.
